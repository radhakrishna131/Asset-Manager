Affiliate Product Discovery Platform – Master Prompt

Build a modern, professional affiliate marketing website that looks and feels like a premium e-commerce platform while acting as a product discovery and redirection system.

Project Overview

Create an affiliate product marketplace where I can paste product URLs from various e-commerce websites. The system should automatically extract product information and generate beautiful product cards.

Users can browse products without creating an account. When they click a product card, they should see a dedicated product details page containing product information, images, specifications, pricing, ratings, and a prominent "Buy Now" button that redirects users to the original affiliate product link.

The platform should prioritize user trust, product discovery, and conversion optimization.
